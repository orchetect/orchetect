## About Me

My name is Steffan Andrews and I hail from beautiful Vancouver, Canada.

I'm a professional [composer for film and TV](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm2862027) with over 19 years of experience specializing in animated series.

I've also been a life-long coder since 1995. I have helped build numerous commercial Mac applications including [Audio Design Desk](https://add.app) and [Dipper](https://dipper.audio). In my spare time I create and maintain open-source packages for Swift (cross-platform) and SwiftUI.
